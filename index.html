About

I'm Habeeb. Most of my work is computer vision in production: medical imaging in hospitals, perception on autonomous vehicles. A lot of it happens at the edge, squeezing transformer detectors and 3D perception onto hardware that isn't a datacenter.

These days my curiosity goes into vision–language models and post-training — where grounding breaks, why VLMs hallucinate so confidently, and whether a model can stay honest about what it actually saw.

I write about what I'm learning, mostly the hard way.

Selected work

  • mathguard alignment · evals

    An RLHF-style alignment pipeline for math LLMs. Harm refusal lifted from 0% to 88% with a 3.2% GSM8K alignment tax; a DistilBERT reward model blocked all 96 jailbreak attempts against a 1.5B model.

  • Rasengan-XL SDXL · LoRA

    PEFT fine-tunes of SDXL 1.0 — LoRA, QLoRA and DoRA — fitting a 2.6B-parameter model on a single 16 GB T4 with 4-bit NF4 and gradient checkpointing.

  • Socio Post Generator LLMs · few-shot

    A few-shot post generator that reads existing posts for style, tags and language, then asks an LLM to write new ones in the same voice.

  • PDF2Podcast Llama 3.2 · TTS

    Turns a dense PDF into a two-speaker dialogue with Llama-3.2-3B and renders it to audio with Kokoro TTS.
